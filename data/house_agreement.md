# 888 State Street — House Agreement

Signed by all four tenants on 1 September 2026. This is the document the house runs
on. Anything not written here is not a house rule, and only a change signed by all
four tenants changes it.

## 2. The lease

- Landlord: Elm City Properties, 42 Trumbull Street.
- Rent: **$3,600 per month**, due on the 1st, late after the 5th.
- Security deposit: $3,600, held by the landlord. Not ours to spend.
- Utilities are not included. The lease runs 1 September 2026 to 31 August 2027.
- Renters insurance is required by the lease. Each tenant buys their own.

## 3. How expenses are split

An expense belongs to one of these categories. The category decides the split.

| Category | Split | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rent | by share | Christina 28%, Sophia 28%, Aletta 22%, Angelika 22% |
| Utilities — electric, gas, water, internet | evenly | Four ways regardless of room size |
| Groceries — shared staples | evenly | Only items on the shared list, section 4 |
| Food — takeaway, restaurants, coffee runs | by item | Whoever ate it. Anything nobody claims stays with the person who paid |
| Household supplies | evenly | Cleaning, paper goods, bulbs, batteries, bin bags |
| Furniture and appliances | evenly | Must be approved in advance, section 6 |
| Repairs the landlord will not cover | evenly | Keep the receipt for the deposit dispute |
| Social — parties, house dinners | evenly | Only if the whole house agreed beforehand |
| **Personal** | **not split** | One person's own food, clothes, medication, hobbies |
| **Guest costs** | **not split** | The host pays, section 8 |
| **Fines and penalties** | **not split** | The person who incurred it pays, section 9 |

If a receipt mixes categories — a grocery run with someone's shampoo in it — the
shared items are split and the personal items are assigned to whoever bought them.
**A mixed receipt is never split in full.**

## 5. Settling up

- The house settles on the **last day of each month**.
- Balances carry over. Nobody is chased mid-month for an ordinary grocery run.
- Anyone whose balance is more than **$150** owed at settle-up should pay within a
  week of the settlement date.
- Payment is by Venmo between tenants. The house does not hold a joint account.

## 6. Approvals

Some things need the house to agree **before** the money is spent:

- Any single expense over **$75** that will be split.
- Any furniture or appliance, at any price.
- Any repair over **$100** the landlord has not agreed to pay for.
- Anything that changes the flat — paint, shelves, anything drilled into a wall.

Agreement means everyone the charge falls on says yes. Three out of four is not
agreement for a four-way charge, whatever the amount. Paying for something counts as
the buyer's own yes, so the buyer does not sign their own charge off — the others do.

Where a charge is split between only some of the house — one person's half of a
takeaway, a repair in one bedroom — the people it is actually split between are the
ones who have to agree. Nobody signs off a charge they are not paying for.

**Rent and the recurring utility bills named in the lease do not need approval.** We
agreed to those when we signed, and asking four people to re-approve the electric bill
every month helps nobody. This exemption covers rent, electric, gas, water and
internet, and nothing else.

An expense over the threshold that was **not** approved in advance is still owed by
the person who bought it, and the house can choose to split it after the fact. It is
not automatically a house expense.

## 7. Chores

A weekly rota, rotating every Sunday:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Christina | Sophia | Aletta | Angelika |
| 2 | Sophia | Aletta | Angelika | Christina |
| 3 | Aletta | Angelika | Christina | Sophia |

- Bins go out **Tuesday and Friday night**. Recycling is Tuesday only.
- A chore is done when it is done, not when it is claimed. Photo evidence settles it.
- Missing your chore twice in a month means you take an extra rotation the next month.
  There is no cash penalty for chores — we tried that in September and it made
  everything worse.

## 8. Guests

- A guest for up to two nights is fine, no notice needed.
- Three nights or more needs a heads-up to the house.
- A guest staying more than **seven nights in a month** pays a **$25 per night**
  contribution toward utilities, paid by the host, not split.
- Anything a guest consumes or breaks is on the host.

## 9. Fines, penalties and damage

Never split. The person responsible pays in full:

- Parking tickets, towing, moving violations.
- Late fees on a bill that person was responsible for paying.
- Damage caused by one person or their guest.
- Anything the landlord deducts from the deposit because of one person's actions.

## 10. Disputes

If someone disagrees with a charge, they say so before the month settles. The charge
is held, not cancelled, while it is discussed. The agreement decides it — not whoever
argues hardest.

A disputed charge needs: who is disputing it, which charge, and why, with the section
of this agreement they think applies. "I don't want to pay for that" is not a dispute.

If the house cannot agree, the charge is split evenly and we move on. Nobody has the
power to delete someone else's charge unilaterally.

## 11. Things that are not house business

- Nobody's individual Venmo history, bank account, or income.
- What anyone earns, owes elsewhere, or spends on themselves.
- Any tenant's contact details beyond what is in section 1, which stays inside the
  house.

## 12. How the house ledger should report

For each expense the ledger records: who paid, the merchant, the date, the total, the
category, which split rule applied, and what each tenant owes as a result. Every
reminder or settlement message is drafted for a person to read and send. **The ledger
never sends anything by itself and never moves money.**
